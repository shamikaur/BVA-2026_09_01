Our Story

A firm built around a specific, recurring problem

Boardview Analytics was founded after years spent inside organizations watching the same pattern repeat: finance builds its own reporting, operations builds its own, HR builds its own, and leadership is left trying to reconcile three versions of the truth before it can make a decision. The problem was rarely a lack of data or effort. It was a lack of a single, trusted way of bringing that data together. Boardview was built to solve that specific problem — directly, practically, and at a scale that fits growing organizations rather than the largest enterprises.

Founder

Shami Kaur, Founder

Shami Kaur founded Boardview Analytics after more than 25 years of leadership experience spanning finance, analytics, workforce planning, and business systems. That background includes hands-on work in FP&A, business intelligence, compensation, HR technology, financial modeling, and enterprise systems — giving Boardview a genuine bridge perspective across finance, operations, and technology, rather than expertise in just one of the three.

That range shapes how Boardview approaches every engagement: understanding the business context first, then designing solutions sized to an organization's actual goals, capabilities, and resources — built to be practical and sustainable well beyond the end of a project.

Our Standard

Practical, not theoretical

Boardview's engagements are judged by one standard: does the organization actually run on what we built. That means hands-on solutions designed with the people who will use them, not delivered from the outside — and it means a preference for the practical fix over the impressive-looking one.
