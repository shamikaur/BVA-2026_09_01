Analytics & Reporting

Turn fragmented reports into one trusted, decision-ready view of performance.

When every department reports differently, leadership spends its time reconciling numbers instead of acting on them. We design reporting that leadership actually trusts — consistent definitions, a single source for key metrics, and a view of the business that holds up under questioning in the boardroom.

What this typically includes:

  • Executive and leadership reporting that consolidates finance, operations, and workforce data into one coherent view
  • Clear definitions and governance for the metrics that matter most, so “revenue” or “headcount” means the same thing everywhere
  • Reporting automation that removes manual consolidation, using tools like Power BI where they fit your environment
  • Self-serve reporting that teams can actually use, so requests stop bottlenecking through one person
FP&A & Performance Management

Build planning and forecasting your leadership team can actually rely on.

Budgets and forecasts lose value fast when they're built once a year and disconnected from how the business actually performs. We help finance and operational leaders build planning processes that stay current, connect to real performance, and give leadership genuine visibility into where the business is headed.

What this typically includes:

  • Budgeting and forecasting processes that update on a realistic cadence, not just once a year
  • Financial modeling that supports real decisions — hiring, investment, expansion — rather than sitting in a spreadsheet unused
  • Workforce and headcount planning connected to the financial plan, not maintained separately
  • Performance management reporting that gives leadership and the board a clear, current view of how the business is tracking
Process & Systems Improvement

Streamline how information moves so reporting scales with the business.

Good reporting depends on what happens upstream of it — how data is captured, moved, and governed. We help organizations redesign the processes and system connections that produce their numbers, so reporting stays reliable as the organization grows.

What this typically includes:

  • Reporting and process redesign that removes manual handoffs and duplicate data entry
  • Data governance that assigns clear ownership and definitions to the metrics leadership depends on
  • System integration and connection work across the platforms you already run, such as NetSuite and Dayforce, so information flows instead of getting re-keyed
  • Scalable operating frameworks designed to hold up as headcount, locations, or business units grow

How engagements typically work

These three areas overlap in practice — a reporting problem is often also a process problem, and a process fix often changes what's possible in FP&A. Most engagements start with a focused assessment of where the business actually stands, then move into implementation and, where useful, ongoing advisory support.
