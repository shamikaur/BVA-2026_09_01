Step 1 of 7

Question 1

Who actually uses this report?

Distribution is not the same as use.

Question 2

What is this report supposed to help you understand or decide?

Try to answer in one or two sentences.

Question 3

What KPIs or measures does the report contain?

Think about the recurring measures leadership is expected to pay attention to.

Question 4

Which KPIs actually influence your discussions, questions, actions, or decisions?

Think about the last few times the report was used.

Question 5

If this report included only half the KPIs tomorrow, which ones would you ask to restore?

Those may be the measures that matter most to you.

What Did You Notice?

A few quick reflections

Are there people receiving the report who do not actively use it?

Can you clearly state what the report helps you or other leaders understand or decide?

Compare the KPIs you listed in Question 3 with the ones you identified in Questions 4 and 5.

Final Reflection

If you could improve just one thing about this report, what would it be?

Do not change anything yet.

Before you go The purpose of this five-minute test is not to prove that your report is good or bad. It is to notice whether what you are producing matches how leadership actually uses it. If the answers surprised you, the report may deserve a closer look.
